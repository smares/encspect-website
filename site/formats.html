Formats and Tags

What Encspect 0.3.0 reads. Each container is read at its own level, and each stream by its frames.

Audio formats

MPEG audio
MP1, MP2 and MP3, with VBR headers, mp3PRO, MP3 Surround and the MPEG-2 multichannel extension. Decoded for measurement (Layers I to III).
AAC
In MP4, ADTS and ADIF, including HE-AAC, HE-AAC v2 and xHE-AAC. Not decoded.
Vorbis, Opus, Speex
In Ogg; Opus also in MP4 and Matroska. Vorbis and Opus are decoded for measurement; Speex is not.
Musepack
SV4 to SV8. Not decoded.
AC-3 and E-AC-3
Raw and in MP4, with the object audio that Dolby Atmos adds. Both decoded for measurement.
DTS
Core, XCh, X96, XXCH and the DTS-HD extension substreams, raw and in MP4, and DTS-HD files (.dtshd). Not decoded.
Windows Media Audio
Versions 1 and 2, Professional, Lossless and Voice, in ASF. Not decoded.
FLAC
Native and in Ogg. Decoded, verifying the audio's MD5, and measured.
WavPack
Lossless, hybrid and DSD, with correction files. Decoded, verifying the CRCs and the MD5, and measured.
Monkey's Audio
All versions parsed; versions 3.80 to 3.99 and 4.11 decoded, verifying every frame's checksum.
TTA
Verified by its CRC-32s and decoded for measurement.
Apple Lossless
Frame by frame in MP4, M4A and Matroska. Decoded for measurement; the format stores no checksum.
Dolby TrueHD and MLP
Raw .thd and .mlp files. Decoded, verifying the lossless checks of the audio.
OptimFROG and TAK
Header level: their blocks or metadata objects with checksums, the stored WAV header and the encoder settings. Not decoded.
PCM in WAV and AIFF
PCM, floating-point, A-law and mu-law samples, decoded for measurement; other codings at header level.

Containers

MP4 and QuickTime
M4A, M4B, 3GP and QuickTime files.
Matroska and WebM
Audio tracks handed to the codec parsers: AAC, AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS, Vorbis, Opus, FLAC, WavPack, Dolby TrueHD, MLP, Apple Lossless and TTA. Video at container level.
Ogg
With Vorbis, Opus, Speex and FLAC.
ASF
WMA, WMV and ASF files.
WAV and Wave64
RIFF, RIFX, RF64, BW64 and Wave64, with all their chunks. MPEG audio, AC-3, E-AC-3 and DTS inside are read by their parsers, also as IEC 61937 bursts.
AIFF
AIFF and AIFF-C, with all their chunks.

Streams that a container encrypts or locks are reported with their scheme and DRM systems. Nothing is decrypted.

Tags

Tag formats that can sit in many kinds of file have parsers of their own. They are found by position and structure, never by a signature alone, because short signatures collide with each other and with audio data. A tag that breaks its specification stays in the report, with findings.

ID3v1 and ID3v2
At the start or end of a file, and inside containers that hold them.
APEv1 and APEv2
At the end of a file, or at the start for APEv2.
Lyrics3 v1 and v2
Before an ID3v1 tag, where its specification places it.
Vorbis comments
In Ogg, FLAC and the other formats that hold them.
Container metadata
Read by the container's own parser and shown as the file's tags: ASF and WMA metadata, the MP4 item list, Matroska's Tags, RIFF INFO in WAV, and the text chunks of AIFF.
